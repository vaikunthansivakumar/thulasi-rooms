About

A home in Nilaveli, not just a room.

We are a small, family-run homestay just off the beach road. You are looked after by the people who actually live here — not checked in at a desk. Settle into a clean room, cook something simple in the kitchen if you feel like it, and spend the evening on the red-tiled veranda with the sea breeze coming through.

Nilaveli’s white sand is a few minutes’ walk away, and most of what is worth seeing on this coast is a short drive. We are always happy to help you plan the day, arrange a tuk-tuk, or point you to a good place to eat.

— the Thulasi family

Nearby

What’s within reach.

Sorted by how far you have to go.

  • 500 m

    Nilaveli Beach

    Wide white sand and calm, clear water — a few minutes on foot from the gate.

  • 1 km

    Pigeon Island

    A marine national park just offshore, with living coral reef. Boats leave from the beach — good snorkelling.

  • Seasonal

    Whale watching

    Morning boat trips out of Trincomalee for blue whales and dolphins. We can help you arrange it.

  • ~20 km

    Kanniya Hot Springs

    Seven natural hot-water wells just outside Trincomalee — an easy stop on the way in or out.

  • ~17 km

    Koneswaram Temple

    An ancient Shiva temple on the cliffs above Trincomalee harbour, worth the trip for the view alone.

  • ~25 km

    Marble Beach

    A quieter, sheltered bay south of town with unusually still water and good sunsets.

Questions

Before you book.

How do I book a room?

Message us on WhatsApp on 076 883 4004 with your dates and how many people are coming. We confirm the room ourselves — there is no booking platform in between, and no commission on top.

What are your rates?

Rates depend on which room you take, whether you want fan or A/C, and the time of year. Message us with your dates and we will send you the current rate straight away.

How far is the beach?

Nilaveli Beach is about 500 metres away — a few minutes on foot, no transport needed.

How do I get to Nilaveli?

Nilaveli sits about 16 km northwest of Trincomalee town along the coast road, roughly half an hour by tuk-tuk or taxi. If you are driving yourself, there is free parking on site.

Do the rooms have air conditioning and hot water?

You can choose a fan-cooled room or an air-conditioned room, whichever suits your budget. Every room has its own bathroom with hot water.

Can I cook my own food?

Yes. There is limited shared kitchen access for simple home cooking, and a shared table to eat at.

Is it suitable for families?

Yes. Every room has two beds, so a family with children fits comfortably in one room. Tell us your group size and we will suggest the best arrangement.
