About us

A home in Nilaveli, not just a room

Thulasi Rooms is a small, family-run homestay tucked just off the beach road in Nilaveli — the kind of place where you're looked after like family, not checked in like a number. Settle into a clean A/C or non-A/C room, make a simple meal with kitchen access, and spend the evening on the red-tiled veranda with the sea breeze coming in.

We're a short walk from Nilaveli's white sand and close to everything else worth seeing on this coast — and we're always happy to help you plan the day.

— the Thulasi family
