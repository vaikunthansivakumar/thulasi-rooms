Shared Spaces

The veranda is the real living room.

Every door in the house opens onto it, so it ends up doing the work a lobby would in a bigger place — except here it's stone benches, a long red-tiled floor, and whoever's around.

There's a shared table to eat at and limited kitchen access if you'd rather cook something simple than go out. Parking is free and on-site, and check-in and check-out are both flexible — tell us your times, even a late bus or an early flight, and we'll work around them.
